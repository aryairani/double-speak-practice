🎧 Double Speak

Two lives, one per ear. Keep them straight.

How it works

  1. Put on headphones. Two short, unrelated life stories play at the same time: one in your left ear, one in your right.
  2. When they finish, retell each story in your own words (type or dictate).
  3. You are graded on completeness (how many details you got), accuracy (how few wrong details you added) and separation (how little you mixed the two stories up).

Headphone check

Each button should be heard on one side only. If they are swapped, flip your headphones.

Stories are fictional. Audio is synthesized with Piper. Grading is deterministic and runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded. How is the score calculated?

Scoring

Each story has a list of 9–11 specific details (name, hometown, job, numbers, pets…). For each ear:

  • Completeness = details you mentioned ÷ total details.
  • Accuracy = correct details ÷ (correct details + details from the other story + numbers that appear in neither story).
  • Separation = 100% minus 25% for every detail that belongs to the other ear.

Ear score = 50% completeness + 30% accuracy + 20% separation. The round score is the average of both ears, multiplied by 0.85 for every extra listen.

Matching is forgiving: plurals, small typos in longer words and spelled-out numbers ("forty-one") all count. Paraphrases of a detail that avoid its key words will not.
